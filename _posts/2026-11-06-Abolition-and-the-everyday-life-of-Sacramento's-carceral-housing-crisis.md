I published this article! This was a difficult one, for sure, as I tried my best to do justice to almost one hundred first-hand stories of homelessness, incarceration, and probation. I have a lot of unanswered questions about what it means to analyze these stories while living in relative comfort. I hope it inspires you to get involved in your community. We need to leverage everything we have to keep people out of incarceration and detention and build safety and sustenance on the outside. Unfortunately I was not able to publish this one open access, but you can [**download the full PDF**](https://miakd.github.io/img/Dawson-Abolition-and-everyday-life.pdf) here, and [**here is the link**](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0962629826001502?dgcid=author) for those with credentials.
