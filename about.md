I'm Mia Karisa Dawson, an urban human geographer and community organizer dedicated to anti-capitalism, anti-imperialism, and abolition. Since recieving my doctorate in Geography in 2023 from the University of California, Davis, I've been awarded a Chancellor’s Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of History at the University of California, Los Angeles and a Postdoctoral Fellowship for Faculty Diversity in the Department of Geography at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County. I study racial relationships of policing, property, and protest that shape U.S. cities. I also work in advocacy to develop approaches to housing and violence prevention by and for people. I also have a background is in environmental justice, and have studied and contributed to advocacy surrounding racial disparities in air and water quality in California.

My work has been published in journals including *Urban Geography*, *EPD: Society and Space*, *Frontiers in Public Health*, *ACME: An International Journal for Critical Geographies*, and *Water Alternatives*. I am inspired and uplifted by many beautiful friends and organizers who I've been fortunate enough to meet on my journey.
